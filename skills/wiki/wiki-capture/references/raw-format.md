# Raw conversation draft

Use a filename containing an ISO date and a short slug. Include:

- capture source and time;
- a concise topic;
- durable facts and decisions;
- unresolved questions;
- useful source or project references;
- privacy-sensitive material removed or generalized.

Keep the draft bounded and suitable for later `wiki-ingest`. Do not include system prompts,
credentials, hidden reasoning, or a transcript dump.
