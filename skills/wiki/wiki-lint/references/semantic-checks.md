# Semantic lint checks

Read candidate page bodies only after deterministic selection.

- Staleness: compare source dates and claims; distinguish old but stable knowledge.
- Contradictions: cite both claims and do not resolve them without evidence.
- Misc promotion: require a stable category identity and useful graph neighborhood.
- Lifecycle/confidence: use the documented evidence, not prose polish.
- Supersession: both pages must identify the relationship and successor.
- Typed relations: flag unsupported causal or dependency semantics.
- Synthesis gaps: require repeated co-occurrence or graph evidence, not topic similarity alone.

Semantic findings are reports until the selected repair mode authorizes the corresponding write.
